# Project Brief: SetuStock NCR

## Problem
Wholesalers and distributors often juggle stock, phone/WhatsApp orders, customer credit, quotations and invoices across notebooks, spreadsheets and disconnected tools. SetuStock brings those workflows into one simple system while keeping WhatsApp as the customer-facing communication layer.

## Primary users
1. Owner: complete visibility and settings
2. Manager: sales, stock, customer and team operations
3. Sales: customers, quotations, orders and collections
4. Warehouse: products, stock and order fulfilment
5. Accountant: invoices, receivables, payments and reports

## MVP scope
- Marketing landing page
- Login and persistent session
- Role based navigation and page guards
- KPI dashboard
- Product/inventory table with low-stock states
- Customer ledger summary
- Order management summary
- WhatsApp customer actions
- Django authentication API
- Django demo data endpoints
- API-first frontend with automatic dummy-data fallback

## Phase 2
- GST invoice PDF generation
- Purchase orders and supplier ledger
- Barcode scanning
- Multi-warehouse stock
- Payment reconciliation
- Quotations to invoice conversion
- WhatsApp Business Platform templates/webhooks
- Audit logs
- Multi-tenant organisations

## Visual direction
Trust-oriented light theme with warm ivory surfaces, deep teal navigation, muted indigo actions, sage success states and restrained saffron highlights. The goal is to feel dependable and calm instead of looking like another electric-blue startup dashboard.
